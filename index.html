Every symbol has one job

Lowercase w is weight per metre. Uppercase W is the pole gap. Lowercase m is slope—not mass, and not the “m” written after a measurement.

Basis, assumptions and retained content

This is an expanded version of cable_slow_visual_calculus_rebuilt.html. All 89 original stages remain in their original order. New micro-steps explain the reasons for the operations, connect earlier results, and add geometric animation demonstrations.

The original image supplies only the pole heights 50 m, cable length 80 m, and lowest-point clearance 10 m. No teaching example replaces those numbers.

The general physical model is a uniform, perfectly flexible, effectively inextensible cable at rest under its own weight, with no intermediate supports or additional loads. Demonstration values a = 2 m, H = 4 N, w = 2 N/m are explicitly illustrative.

The exact result for the image remains unchanged: no positive-span smooth catenary exists for those exact data. The puzzle's zero gap is a degenerate limit.

The following references were included in the previous version and are retained for the underlying standard results. The new causal explanations and geometric constructions are worked through in this file.

OpenStax Calculus Volume 2, §2.4 — arc length from chord approximations and Pythagoras.

NASA Glenn, Control Line Equations — uniform-cable catenary model.

NIST DLMF §4.28 — exponential definitions; §4.34 — derivatives; §4.35 — identities.
