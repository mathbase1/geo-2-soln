The lesson is divided into small clicks. The difficult integral and both exponential integrals are evaluated explicitly, then checked by differentiation.

Basis and references

Given data: the original image supplied in the conversation: 50 m pole heights, 80 m total cable length, and 10 m ground clearance. The original values are never changed without an explicit “changed example” label.

Model assumptions: uniform weight per actual cable length; flexible, inextensible cable; static gravity loading; no other supports or loads. These are stated assumptions, not extra measurements in the picture.

Arc length: OpenStax Calculus Volume 2, §2.4 develops arc length through straight-segment approximations and Pythagoras.

Cable mechanics: NASA Glenn, Control Line Equations, for the uniformly weighted self-weight catenary model.

Function definitions: NIST DLMF §4.28, the exponential definitions of sinh and cosh.

Derivative checks: NIST DLMF §4.34.

The diagrams, detailed intermediate algebra, checks, and the application to the original numbers have been worked out in this lesson. No internet connection is required to use the lesson itself.
